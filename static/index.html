How to play

Be the last player with influence. Character actions and blocks are claims: you may bluff, and either player may challenge.

  • Income: gain 1 coin.
  • Foreign Aid: gain 2; the Duke can block.
  • Tax (Duke): gain 3 coins.
  • Steal (Captain): take up to 2 coins.
  • Exchange (Ambassador): draw 2, then return 2.
  • Assassinate (Assassin): pay 3 to remove influence.
  • Coup: pay 7 to remove influence; at 10 coins it is required.
